Cleartable vs Todoist: Which Task App Is Right for You?

October 6, 2026 · 7 min read · by the Cleartable team

Todoist is one of the most popular task managers in the world, and for good reason. It's fast, reliable, and works everywhere. Millions of people trust it to manage their work and personal tasks.

Cleartable takes a different approach. Instead of giving you a blank list and letting you organize everything yourself, it uses AI to do the sorting, filing, and planning for you. It also bundles habit tracking and a calendar view into the same app, so you don't need three separate tools.

This isn't a hit piece. Both apps are good at different things. Here's an honest comparison to help you figure out which one fits the way you work.

Where Todoist wins

Let's be honest about what Todoist does better:

  • Maturity and trust. Todoist has been around since 2007. It's battle-tested, polished, and works on every platform. If you need something that just works on day one with no surprises, Todoist is hard to beat.
  • Team features. Shared projects, task comments, and assignments make Todoist a real option for small teams. Cleartable is a solo tool today.
  • Integrations. Todoist connects to practically everything — Slack, email, IFTTT, Zapier, calendar apps, and more. Cleartable currently connects to Google Calendar and that's it.
  • Native mobile apps. Todoist's iOS and Android apps are fast and native. Cleartable offers a PWA, which works well but isn't quite the same experience.
  • Natural language input. Todoist's date parsing ("every third Thursday", "tomorrow at 3pm p1") is excellent and has years of refinement behind it.

Where Cleartable wins

Cleartable shines in a few places Todoist doesn't reach:

  • AI that actually organizes. Todoist parses dates from text. Cleartable goes further — it reads your plain-language thought, figures out the project, priority, context, and due date, and files the task for you. You confirm with one tap instead of filling in fields.
  • AI daily planning. Every morning, Cleartable looks at your calendar, your overdue tasks, and your priorities, then builds a realistic plan for the day — fitting tasks into the gaps between your meetings. Todoist shows you a list; Cleartable helps you decide what to do with it.
  • Habit tracking, built in. If you track habits, you currently need a separate app alongside Todoist (Habitica, Streaks, Loop). Cleartable puts tasks and habits in the same place, with streaks, skip days, intervals, and even quit-habit tracking.
  • Calm over power. Todoist is a power tool with a learning curve — filters, labels, priority levels, natural language syntax. Cleartable is deliberately simpler. AI handles the complexity so you don't have to learn a system.
  • Weekly review. GTD's weekly review is the habit that keeps any task system alive. Cleartable has it built in, AI-assisted. Todoist leaves it up to you.
  • Price. Cleartable Pro is $4/month ($36/year). Todoist Pro is $5/month ($48/year). Both have a free tier.

Pick Todoist if…

You want a proven, full-featured task manager with native apps on every platform, team collaboration, and an ecosystem of integrations. You prefer to organize things yourself and you've already built a workflow that works. You need something your whole team can use.

Pick Cleartable if…

You want AI to do the organizing so you can just capture and go. You track habits alongside tasks and don't want two apps. You like the idea of a daily plan built around your calendar. You want a calm, simple interface that doesn't ask you to learn a system — it learns yours.

Try both — no migration needed

You don't have to choose today. Cleartable is free to start and there's nothing to import. Capture your next few tasks there and see if the AI-first workflow clicks. If it does, great. If Todoist is still your home, no harm done.

Frequently asked questions

Is Cleartable a good alternative to Todoist?

Cleartable is a strong alternative if you want built-in AI that organizes tasks for you, integrated habit tracking, and calendar-aware daily planning in one app. If you need team collaboration, dozens of integrations, or a mature mobile app ecosystem, Todoist is the safer choice.

Can I switch from Todoist to Cleartable?

Yes. You can start using Cleartable alongside Todoist — there's nothing to migrate. Capture your new tasks in Cleartable and keep Todoist for existing projects. Most people know within a week whether the AI-first workflow suits them.

Is Cleartable free?

Cleartable has a free tier that includes up to 8 projects, 1 custom list, 3 habits, and AI-powered capture. Pro ($4/month or $36/year) unlocks unlimited projects, habits, analytics, and higher AI limits.
